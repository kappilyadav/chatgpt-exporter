## Full read-through (for recording in one take)
Meet ChatGPT Exporter. Save any chat, in one click.

Open any conversation on ChatGPT, click Export, and choose your format. PDF, Word, Markdown, HTML, text, JSON, CSV, or even an image. Eight formats. One menu.

Don't need the whole chat? Open Select and tick exactly the messages you want. Questions only, answers only, or pick your own.

Make it yours. Choose page size, theme, margins, fonts, page numbers, and a table of contents. Light or dark, it's your call.

Code blocks, tables, and math come out perfectly formatted, ready to share.

And it's private. Everything runs inside your browser. Your chats never touch our servers.

ChatGPT Exporter. Free on the Chrome Web Store.

## Notes
- Say "ChatGPT Exporter" as three even syllables groups: "Chat-G-P-T Ex-por-ter".
- If a line runs long, drop the last list in that sentence (for example, say "PDF, Word, Markdown, and more").
- The sentence "never touch our servers" is accurate for chat content. An optional account check only asks the website whether your plan is active.
- A soft, upbeat background track at about -22 dB works well. Use royalty-free music only.
